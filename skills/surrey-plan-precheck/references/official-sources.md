## Notes

- Surrey says consolidated bylaws are convenience copies and certified versions/original amendments may be needed for legal purposes.
- The Province states BCBC 2024 came into effect March 8, 2024. Earthquake and adaptable dwelling requirements have separate effective-date/in-stream rules.
- BCBC content is copyrighted by the Province of B.C. and National Research Council portions. Do not copy the full code into this skill or generated reports.
